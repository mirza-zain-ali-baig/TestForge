# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Educators, teachers, academy directors, and academic administrators in secondary and higher-secondary educational institutions (Matriculation, Intermediate FSc/ICS, O/A-Levels, Cambridge International). Teachers operating on smartphones and tablets on-the-go to create quick assessments, browse questions, and practice interactively, as well as desktop workstations for bulk printing and school exports.

## Product Purpose
ExamForge AI resolves administrative bottlenecks in examination compilation: eliminates manual multi-hour paper preparation, avoids cognitive bias toward rote recall, enforces syllabus fidelity, prevents examination paper leaks via disjoint anti-cheat multi-variant generation (Sets A–F), and provides full mobile/desktop WYSIWYG paper preview and export (.docx, PDF, browser print).

## Positioning
Hybrid deterministic curriculum traversal (2,123+ pre-indexed curriculum questions) coupled with autonomous generative synthesis (Google Gemini 2.5 Flash / OpenRouter AI) and mathematical disjoint multi-variant partitioning ($V_i \cap V_j = \emptyset$) for leak-proof exam generation.

## Operating Context
Mobile web and desktop browsers. Teachers operating on mobile devices in classrooms, staff rooms, and commutes requiring touch-friendly ergonomic controls, thumb-zone navigation, single-column vertical flows, and instant interactive feedback; alongside desktop environments for bulk Word/PDF exam generation with academy watermarks.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Multi-tier curriculum hierarchy (Class -> Subject -> Chapter -> Topic).
- Dual generation engines: Deterministic Question Bank & AI Synthesis (Gemini/OpenRouter).
- Multi-variant anti-cheat paper generation (up to 6 parallel disjoint variants A-F).
- Responsive mobile UX: bottom navigation bar, thumb-friendly touch targets (min 44x44px), drawer menus, collapsible filters, mobile-optimized question cards, and full-screen AI chat assistant.
- Interactive online test practice mode with instant scoring and explanations.
- Question Bank search, filtering, bookmarking, and custom teacher notes.
- True multi-format export: formatted DOCX, A4 paginated print layout, PDF download.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: ExamForge AI
- Palette: Authoritative Emerald (#059669 / #10b981), Slate, and Clean Teal accents.
- Typography: Geist Sans, Geist Mono, KaTeX for mathematical rendering.

## Evidence on Hand
- Full database of 2,123+ curriculum questions with taxonomy, difficulty, and chapter indexing.
- Implemented Next.js 16 + React 19 app shell with mobile bottom nav, generator wizard, question bank, test preview, chat assistant, history, and saved papers.

## Product Principles
1. Curriculum Fidelity: Generated questions strictly adhere to approved syllabus topics and mark allocations.
2. Mobile Parity & Accessibility: Every feature, from blueprint configuration to paper editing and AI tutoring, must be fully accessible and ergonomically seamless on touch and small screens without horizontal scrolling or truncation.
3. Deterministic & Anti-Cheat Rigor: Question variants must guarantee zero unintended option leakage across sets while maintaining strict cognitive balance across Bloom's Taxonomy.
4. Clean Production Craft: Solid color hierarchy, 44px+ touch targets, no decorative gradient text or faux borders, high contrast and legible typography.
